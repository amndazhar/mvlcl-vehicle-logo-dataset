# Privacy and ethics

## Approach: a documentation-only showcase

This repository documents how the dataset was built and checked. It **does not distribute the dataset**.

| Published here | Not published |
|---|---|
| Methodology and documentation | Original CCTV recordings or trimmed clips |
| Aggregate statistics and charts | Extracted frames, at any resolution, anonymised or not |
| Processing, anonymisation and QC code | Label files tied to restricted images |
| Synthetic illustrations (drawn scenes) | Source metadata (`video_info.csv`), review pages, detection logs |
| | Site name, location, camera identifiers, recording dates |
| | Model weights trained on the restricted data |

## Why blurring is not enough

- **Permission.** The footage was obtained from the management of a residential community. Permission covered the academic project on the condition that the community isn't named. Permission to **redistribute** the footage is **not documented**, and anonymisation does not create it.
- **Residual identifiability.** Vehicle make and colour, stickers or damage, the site layout and the time of day can still link a vehicle to a household, even with plates and faces removed. CCTV of a residential entrance reveals people's movements.
- **Imperfect automation.** Automatic detectors miss some plates and faces; see [anonymisation-methodology.md](anonymisation-methodology.md#known-limitations).

## Safeguards applied

- No image, clip, frame crop or thumbnail of the footage is committed. All visual examples are drawn synthetic scenes, generated by [`visualizations/generate_synthetic_figures.py`](../visualizations/generate_synthetic_figures.py).
- Charts show **aggregate** values only: counts, distributions and normalised box positions.
- Identifying text in the project's own records (site name, camera IDs, dates, storage links) is excluded.
- `.gitignore` blocks image, video, model-weight and archive files.
- The anonymisation pipeline removes overlay text, plates and faces, and strips metadata. It's provided so future releases, if ever permitted, can be prepared consistently.

## Legal context (not legal advice)

The footage was recorded in Malaysia. The **Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA)** and the data owner's terms are the relevant frameworks. Before any data release:
- obtain **written** permission from the data owner that explicitly covers redistribution and its scope (who, what, how)
- confirm with the university whether its intellectual-property policy covers project outputs
- get an independent review of anonymisation quality.

## Ethical considerations

- **Purpose limitation.** The dataset supports research on recognising vehicle *brands*. It is not designed for, and must not be used for, identifying or tracking individuals or vehicles.
- **Trademarks.** Vehicle logos are trademarks of their owners and are referred to only for research description.
- **Contact.** Concerns about this repository can be raised through its issue tracker.
